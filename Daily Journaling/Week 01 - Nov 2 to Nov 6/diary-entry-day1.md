## 🧩 Data vs Information
- **Data**: Raw facts, figures, or symbols without context.
- **Information**: Processed data that carries meaning and supports decision-making.

**Example:**  
- Data: `42`, `"apple"`, `2026-11-03`  
- Information: *“Muruganantham bought 42 apples on November 3, 2026.”*

---

## 🗄️ What is a Database?
- A **database** is an organized collection of data that allows efficient storage, retrieval, and management.
- Acts like a digital filing system for structured information.

---

## 📚 Types of Databases
- **Relational (RDBMS)** – Tables, rows, columns; SQL-based.  
- **NoSQL** – Schema-less, flexible for unstructured/semi-structured data.  
- **Hierarchical** – Tree-like parent-child structure.  
- **Network** – Graph-like relationships.  
- **Object-oriented** – Stores data as objects.  
- **Cloud databases** – Hosted, scalable, managed services.  
- **Graph databases** – Focused on relationships and connections.

---

## 📂 Structured vs Unstructured Data
- **Structured Data**: Organized in fixed formats (tables, rows, columns).  
  *Example: Customer names, phone numbers, transactions.*
- **Unstructured Data**: No predefined format, harder to store in traditional databases.  
  *Example: Emails, videos, social media posts.*
- **Semi-structured Data**: Partially organized (JSON, XML).
