- What does **SUM** do?  
- What does **AVERAGE** do?  
- What is the difference between **COUNT** and **COUNTA**?  
- What does **MIN** return?  
- What does **MAX** return?  
- How do these functions behave with:
  - Empty cells  
  - Text

- How do these functions help in business analysis?
  - Total sales  
  - Average salary  
  - Minimum stock level  
  - Maximum revenue  
- What is the difference between:
  - AVERAGE  
  - MEDIAN  
  - MODE  
- How do you combine these functions with references?  
- How do these functions behave inside Excel Tables?  
- Why are these functions essential for KPI calculations?
