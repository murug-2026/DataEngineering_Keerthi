## 📘 **Day 4: Data Lifecycle, Data Sources, Data Flow & Business Applications**

### **Conceptual Questions**
- What is the **data lifecycle** (creation → storage → usage → archival → deletion)?  
- Why is understanding the lifecycle important in data science and business intelligence?  
- What are the main **data sources** in business (databases, spreadsheets, APIs, sensors, logs)?  
- How does data flow from **source systems** to **reporting systems**?  
- What is the difference between **operational data** and **analytical data**?  
- How do businesses use data in **applications** like CRM, ERP, HR, finance, healthcare?  
- What are common challenges in data flow (duplication, latency, inconsistency)?  
- How does ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) fit into the data lifecycle?

### **Practical Thinking Questions**
- Can you map the lifecycle of data for a retail store (sales → reporting)?  
- How would you identify the data sources in a hospital system?  
- What happens if data flow is broken at one stage?  
- How do business applications depend on clean and timely data?

### **Hands-On Practice Questions**
- Draw a simple **data flow diagram** for a business (e.g., online shopping).  
- Identify **data sources** for a small project (Excel file, CSV, API).  
- Map how data moves from source → transformation → reporting.  

### **Reflection Questions**
- Can you explain the lifecycle of data in your own words?  
- Can you identify at least 3 data sources around you?  
- Can you sketch a simple business data flow without help?
