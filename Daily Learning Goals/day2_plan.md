## 🎯 **Day 2 Learning Questions (Full-Day Agenda)**  
### **1. Data — What You Must Understand**
- What is **data** in the simplest sense?  
- What are the **different types of data** (numeric, categorical, text, time-series, etc.)?  
- What is the difference between **qualitative** and **quantitative** data?  
- What is the difference between **raw data** and **processed data**?  
- Why is data considered the **foundation** of data science?  
- What makes data **good** or **bad** (accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness)?

---

### **2. Information — Turning Data Into Meaning**
- What is **information**, and how is it different from data?  
- How does data become information?  
- What is **context**, and why is it important for converting data into information?  
- What are examples where the same data produces **different information** depending on context?  
- Why do businesses rely on information rather than raw data?

---

### **3. Databases — Where Data Lives**
- What is a **database**, and why do we need one?  
- What problems arise if we store data in Excel instead of a database?  
- What is a **DBMS** (Database Management System)?  
- What is the difference between **database** and **DBMS**?  
- What are the main types of databases?
  - Relational (SQL)
  - NoSQL (Document, Key-value, Columnar, Graph)
- What is a **table**, **row**, **column**, **primary key**, **foreign key**?  
- What is **schema** in a database?  
- Why do relational databases enforce **structure**?  
- Why do NoSQL databases allow **flexibility**?

---

### **4. Structured vs Unstructured Data**
- What is **structured data**?  
- What is **unstructured data**?  
- What is **semi-structured data** (JSON, XML)?  
- Why do companies generate more unstructured data today?  
- What tools handle structured data well (SQL databases)?  
- What tools handle unstructured data well (NoSQL, Hadoop, cloud storage)?  
- Why is unstructured data harder to analyze?  
- How does structured data help in **machine learning**?

---

## 🧠 **5. Practical Thinking Questions (Very Important for Data Science Mindset)**
- How would you convert unstructured data (text, logs, images) into structured form?  
- How do you decide which database type to use for a project?  
- How does data quality impact machine learning models?  
- What are examples of structured data in real life (banking, retail, healthcare)?  
- What are examples of unstructured data in real life (emails, social media, sensor logs)?  
- Why do data scientists spend **80% of time** cleaning and preparing data?

---

## 🛠️ **6. Hands-On Practice Questions (You should attempt these today)**
### **Data & Information**
- Given a list of numbers, how will you identify:
  - min, max, average, median  
- Given a raw text paragraph, how will you extract meaningful information?

### **Structured Data**
- Create a small table of students with columns:
  - id, name, age, marks  
- Identify:
  - primary key  
  - duplicate rows  
  - missing values  

### **Unstructured Data**
- Take a sample email or WhatsApp message:
  - Extract sender  
  - Extract date  
  - Extract main message  
  - Convert it into structured format (table)

### **Database Thinking**
- Design a simple database for:
  - Library  
  - Hospital  
  - Online shopping  
- Identify tables, columns, relationships.
