| Day   | 3 Hours: Concepts + Documentation                                                | 2 Hours: Hands-on Activity           |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Day 1 | Introduction to IT industry, data-related careers, data engineering vs analytics | Explore sample datasets              |

* Data fundamentals documentation.
* Three sample datasets.
